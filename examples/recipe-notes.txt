Title: Pan paneer & peppers
Ingredients:
Paneer | 200 g
Capsicum | 1
Curd | 2 tbsp
Cumin | Not stated
Steps:
Mix paneer with curd and cumin.
Slice the capsicum.
Cook everything in a pan until the capsicum softens.
Equipment:
No oven is needed; use a pan.
